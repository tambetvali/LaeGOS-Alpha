### 12.1. Normalized linear-exponential comparison

Define:

$$
f(x)=a^x
$$

$$

$$
f(0)=g(0)

and:

$$
f'(0)=g'(0)

$$
\Delta(x)=a^x-1-x\ln(a)

$$
