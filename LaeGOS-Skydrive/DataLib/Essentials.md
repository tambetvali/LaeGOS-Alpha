# A Revised Principle

The previous article ended with:

```text
Even
    → symmetry
    → space
    → horizontality

Odd
    → hierarchy
    → scale
    → verticality
```

This article suggests a refinement:

```text
Even
    → symmetry appears internally

Odd
    → symmetry returns externally
```

and

```text
Phi
    → symmetry never fully settles
    → balance appears through continual adjustment
```

Thus we obtain three very different geometries:

```text
4
    coordinate geometry

3
    rank geometry

φ
    transition geometry
```

where coordinates generate space, ranks generate hierarchy, and phi generates the visible "noise" between compatible spaces. In a hologram framework that noise may not be an imperfection at all. It may be the signature of dimensions attempting to become mutually visible.
