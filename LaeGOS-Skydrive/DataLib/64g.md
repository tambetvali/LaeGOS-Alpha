## 2.1. Rapidity coordinates

Define:

$$
\eta_n=n\ln 2
$$

The corresponding relativistic velocity is:

$$
v_n=c\tanh(n\ln 2)
$$

The Lorentz factor is:

$$
\gamma_n=\cosh(n\ln 2)
$$

Or:

$$
\gamma_n=\frac{2^n+2^{-n}}{2}
$$

This creates exact symmetry:

$$
v_{-n}=-v_n
$$

$$
\gamma_{-n}=\gamma_n
$$

The velocity approaches light speed without exceeding it.

This is a useful mathematical mapping between signed Laegna positions and relativistic motion.

## 2.2. Physical interpretation of complex coordinates

$$
\mathcal{V}=v_{\parallel}+iv_{\perp}

| Component | Physical name | Scope |

$$
