# Shape Coding

| Symbol | Meaning |
|:---:|:---:|
| 🤍 | ***Sign is half (b2) or one quarter (b4) of directional encoding; for example + and - are two directions; heart is symbol of how starting from neutral zero, one can follow the mean or divide the axe to two infinities.*** |
| ⬜ | ***Circle at second octave is topologically most similar to square; square thus means even octave or two-channel content.*** |
| ⚪ | ***Circle is the normal reach of a scalar - if squares are even, this is odd; if square is two-channel - this is one.*** |
