## Numbers

Numbers through octaves:
- If lower octave forms circle, the higher forms square - square is best representation for it tries to resolve limits within a repeated area.
  - Circle, from outside, looks square; square, from outside, looks circle.
  - In LaeChing, in trigrams 8, square and circle come in one, in hexagrams 64, in another - because higher octave of 8 must match the lower of 64, they have to be reverse-directional.
  - For this reason, sometimes the naming of first and second group of four could be switched to show relative positioning in curvature layers and levels. On first hyperoctave, circle seems larger; on second, square seems larger - then, even orientation is color-coding things.
