# Laegna Futhark

> A primitive coordinate grammar for tracking zeroes, identities, projections, and reprojections across rank spaces.

## Introduction

The Laegna Futhark is one of the simplest symbolic structures in Laegna Mathematics.

It is not primarily an alphabet.

It is not primarily a number system.

It is not primarily a geometric figure.

Instead, it is a map of how origins behave when coordinate systems transform.

It describes:

- moving zeroes,
- moving identities,
- exponent-linearization,
- logarithm-linearization,
- coordinate compression,
- coordinate recovery,
- hologram projection.

At its simplest, the Laegna Futhark may be drawn as:

```text
  2   2
   \ /
    1
    |
    0
    |
    1
   / \
  2   2
```

The figure is symmetric.

Yet the upper and lower halves are not identical.

They represent opposite viewpoints of the same structure.

## Why "Futhark"

Historically, *Futhark* is the name of the ancient runic alphabet.

Its letters were primitive symbolic building blocks from which larger structures emerged.

The Laegna Futhark follows a similar role.

Instead of generating words, it generates coordinate systems.

Instead of letters, it uses primitive relations.

Instead of language, it describes transformations of space.

The Laegna Futhark therefore functions as:

> A coordinate alphabet.

The simplest meaningful relations repeatedly found throughout the system.
