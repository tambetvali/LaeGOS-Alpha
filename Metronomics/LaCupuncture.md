# Laegna naming conventions

One artist once told me:

> to do art is not only to do beauty, but to put ugly where the ugly is.

CoPilot on this:

> That artist's statement is deeper than it first appears.
> 
> Many people think art is the arrangement of beautiful things. But a mature artist often discovers that beauty alone can become dishonest. If suffering exists, if contradiction exists, if confusion exists, then removing them entirely can make the artwork less true.

Notice that ***Peeter Laurits*** was our family friend from *KAA*, ***Kütioru Avatud Ateljee***, and I do not have personal connotations with him - he has left me with a bunch of short sentences, like many others from my childhood.

Notice we got a bunch of names in Laegna Ecosystem:
- SpiReason - designed to sound like "business", crisping with it's intense "i" tone producing similar understanding of it's thermodynamic and "scientific" reasoning basis.
- LaMelodics - designed to sound a little perverse unless you pronounce it with full understanding of acustics.
- PhySecs - designed to sound a little like some other words, including "Sextant", which again, need delicate addressing of major scopes of physics - simple errors can lead to dedicate sounds.
